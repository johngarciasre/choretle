# Choretle — Kid User Guide

Welcome! 🌟 You're about to learn how to earn points, complete chores, and keep your streak alive. Let's go!

---

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Open **Choretle** and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your name, email, and password.
3. Join your family using the code your parent shared with you.
4. You're in! 🎉

### Signing In
- Go to **Sign In** and enter your email and password.
- Your parent can also set up a quick login link so you don't have to type everything every time.

---

## My Chores — Your Personal Chore Page

When you open **My Chores**, you'll see three things:

### 1. Affirmation Banner (Top)
A colorful banner with an encouraging message based on your progress:
- "Nice work finishing a chore! Keep it up!" if you just completed something
- "5+ chores this week — amazing work!" if you're doing great
- "Ready to tackle some chores today?" if you haven't started yet

Your **total points** and **streak days** are shown right here too.

### 2. Recently Completed (Middle)
A list of the chores you've finished recently, showing:
- What chore you did
- How many points you earned ⭐
- When you finished it ("30m ago", "2d ago")

### 3. Next Up — Your Coming Tasks (Bottom)
Up to three tasks are shown in a horizontal carousel. Each card shows:
- Task name and description
- Points you'll earn ⭐
- Due date (if set)
- Whether it's "In Progress" or waiting for you

**Tap a task card** to open its details page.

---

## Your Tasks — The Kanban Board

Go to **My Chores** → scroll down to see your full **Kanban board**. It has three columns:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Todo** 📝 | Tasks waiting for you |
| **Doing** ⚧ | Tasks you're working on right now |
| **Done** ✅ | Tasks you've finished |

### How to Move a Task
- **Start a task**: Drag it from **Todo → Doing** (or tap "Start Task")
- **Continue a task**: Stay in **Doing** and tap "Continue" to see details
- **Finish a task**: Drag it from **Doing → Done** (or mark it done on the detail page)

When you drag a task to **Done**, you earn your points! ⭐

### Task Detail Page
Tap any task to see:
- Full description and due date
- Points available
- Status change options (start, continue, finish)
- History of status changes

## Badges & Medals — Your Achievements

As you complete chores, you earn badges and medals:

### Badges
| Badge | How to Earn It |
|-------|---------------|
| 🏿 First Task | Complete your first chore |
| 👷 Hard Worker | Finish 5 chores total |
| 💎 Point Master | Earn 100 points |
| 🏆 Champion | Earn 500 points |

### Medals
Medals are earned based on your performance in different categories:
- **Bronze** — First level of achievement
- **Silver** — Intermediate achievement
- **Gold** — Impressive achievement
- **Diamond** — Top-tier achievement (the best!)

You can see all your earned badges and medals on the **My Chores** page.

## Dashboard — Your Quick Overview

Your dashboard shows:
- **Total Points** — All points you've ever earned
- **Day Streak** — How many days in a row you've completed at least one chore
- **Done (7d)** — How many chores you finished this week
- **Pending** — How many tasks are waiting for you

### Your Tasks Section
Shows all your outstanding (not yet done) tasks with:
- Task name and description
- Due date badge (red if overdue!)
- Points available
- "Start Task" button to begin working on it

### Needs Verification
Sometimes you might mark a task as done, but your parent needs to approve it first. These show up in the "Needs Verification" section with a ⚧ icon.

## Profile — Your Stats & History

Go to **Profile** (from the dashboard or profile dropdown) to see:

### Hero Card
- Your name and role
- Total points badge ⭐
- Current streak 🔪

### Stats
| Stat | What It Means |
|------|--------------|
| Total Points | All-time points earned |
| Jobs Done | Total chores completed |
| Avg / Job | Average points per chore |
| Best Streak | Longest streak you've ever had |

### 7-Day Activity Chart
A bar chart showing how many points you earned each of the last 7 days. Green bars = busy days! 🟧

### Categories
Shows which types of chores you do most (e.e., "Kitchen" = 40 pts, "Cleaning" = 30 pts).

### Points History Timeline
A timeline of your recent completions with points and dates.

### Badges Section
All badge achievements — earned ones are colorful, unearned ones are grayed out with a target icon.

## Tips for Earning More Points 🌟

1. **Complete chores quickly** — Some tasks give more points when done early.
2. **Keep your streak alive** — Do at least one chore every day to maintain your streak.
3. **Try different categories** — Earn badges across multiple chore types.
4. **Check "Next Up" regularly** — New tasks appear as slates generate lists.
5. **Drag to Done carefully** — Only mark a task done when it's truly finished!
