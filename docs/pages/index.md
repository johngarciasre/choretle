# Home (Landing Page)

The home page at `/` is the first thing visitors see. It introduces Choretle and provides a call-to-action to get started.

## What You See

- A hero section with the Choretle logo, tagline ("Chores, points & fun for the whole family"), and a **Get Started** button.
- Three feature cards:
  - **Set It Up Once** — Create your family, add chores, and assign them in minutes.
  - **Kids Love It** — Earn points, build streaks, and climb the leaderboard.
  - **Rotate & Reward** — Fair chore rotation, drag-and-drop boards, and a swap meet marketplace.
- A testimonial tagline at the bottom.

## How to Use

1. Click **Get Started** to go to the Sign In page.
2. If you already have an account, sign in. Otherwise, create one (see Sign In / Sign Up docs).
3. Once authenticated, you will land on the Dashboard and can begin setting up your family.
