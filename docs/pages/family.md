# Family

The Family page is the central hub for managing your family's settings, members, teams, tags, and appearance.

## Creating or Joining a Family (First-Time Users)

If you are new to Choretle:

1. On the Family page you will see two options:
   - **Create Family** — Enter a name for your family and click "Create Family".
   - **Join Family** — Click "Join Family", enter the join code shared by an existing family, and click "Join".
2. After creating or joining, you are taken to the full Family settings page for your family.

## Managing Family Settings

### General Settings

- **Family Name** — Click the pencil icon next to the name to edit it. Press Enter or click Save to confirm.
- **Timezone** — Displayed read-only; set at creation time.
- **Week Starts On** — Which day of the week your family's tracking weeks begin (Sunday through Saturday).

### Theme

Choose a color theme for your family's UI:
- Click one of the colored circles (Coral, Teal, Sunny, Grape, Bubblegum) to select it.
- The change is applied immediately across all pages.

## Managing Members

Family members are listed with their name, email, role, total points, and team membership. You can view existing members here; new members are added when they sign up and are assigned to your family.

## Managing Teams (Optional)

If teams are enabled for your family:

### Creating a Team

1. Click **Create Team**.
2. Enter a team name and optionally a logo URL.
3. Click **Create Team**.

### Assigning Members to Teams

1. Select a team from the dropdown.
2. Select a user from the member dropdown.
3. Click **Assign User**.

### Toggling Teams

To enable or disable team management:
1. Click "Toggle Teams" in the settings.
2. Check or uncheck the "Enable team management" box.
3. Click Save Changes.

## Managing Tags

Tags are used to categorize tasks. They appear as colored pills on task cards and can be used for auto-inclusion rules in slates.

### Creating a Tag

1. In the Tags section, click **Add Tag**.
2. Enter a tag name and pick a color.
3. Click **Create Tag**.

### Editing a Tag

- Click the **pencil icon** below any tag to edit its name or color.

### Deleting a Tag

- Click the **trash icon** below any tag. Confirm in the dialog. Deleted tags are not removed from existing tasks — the task simply loses that tag association.
