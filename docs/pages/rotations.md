# Rotations

The Rotations page is where you assign chores to family members fairly using a drag-and-drop board. Each column on the board represents a slate, and each row within a column is a day in the rotation schedule.

## What You See

- A board with columns (one per slate) and rows representing days or rotation periods.
- Family member cards that can be dragged between slates and positions.
- Assignment counts showing how many total assignments exist across all slates.
- The family name displayed in the header.

## How to Use

### Assigning a Chore Rotation

1. **Drag** a family member card from one position to another on the board.
2. Drop them onto the desired slate column and day slot.
3. The assignment is saved automatically.

### Removing an Assignment

- Click the **X** button on any assignment card, or drag it out of the board area to remove that person's chore for that slot.

### Understanding Fairness

The rotation system ensures fair distribution:
- Each family member rotates through slates over a configurable interval.
- The board lets you see and adjust the current schedule at a glance.
- If someone has too many or too few assignments, simply drag them to a different position.

### Configuring Rotation Intervals

Each slate has an interval setting (e.e., every 7 days). This controls how frequently the assignment shifts to the next person in the rotation order. You can adjust this when creating or editing a slate.
