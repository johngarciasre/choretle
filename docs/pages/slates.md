# Slates

A **Slate** (short for "slate" — a blend of slate and state) is a chore template or set. It groups multiple tasks together so they can be rotated as a unit across family members. Think of it as a category like "Kitchen Cleaning" or "Bathroom Sanitation" that contains specific tasks.

## What You See

- A grid of slate cards showing each slate's name, description, frequency (e.e., weekly, biweekly), interval, and active status.
- The number of tasks associated with each slate.
- A floating **+** button to create a new slate.
- Each card has a delete icon (trash) that appears on hover.

## How to Use

### Creating a New Slate

1. Click the **+** floating button (bottom-right corner).
2. Enter a name for the slate (e.e., "Kitchen Cleaning").
3. Press Enter or click **Create Slate**.

### Configuring Tasks in a Slate

Each slate can contain tasks in two ways:

1. **Explicit Tasks** — Specific tasks that must always be included and manually marked as complete.
2. **Auto-Include by Tags** — Rules that automatically pull in any task matching selected tags.

To configure:

1. On the slate card, click **Configure Tasks & Tags**.
2. You will see three sections:
   - **Explicit Tasks** — Tasks that are always included. Use the **Remove** button to take tasks out.
   - **Auto-Include by Tags** — Select tags; any task with a matching tag is automatically added.
   - **Effective Tasks (Total)** — A preview of all tasks that will be generated by combining explicit and auto-included tasks.
3. Click **Save Slate Configuration** when done.

### Deleting a Slate

1. Hover over the slate card.
2. Click the **trash icon** in the top-right corner.
3. Confirm deletion in the dialog. Note: This also removes all associated rotations and task assignments.

### Why Slates Matter

Slates are the foundation of your rotation schedule. When you go to **Rotations**, each column on the board represents a slate — and family members are dragged onto those columns to receive their assigned chores.
