# Swap Meet

Swap Meet lets you share your chore slate templates with other families, so they can import and use your pre-configured chores in their own rotation schedules.

## What You See

- **Rotation Schedule** — A calendar-style view showing upcoming chore assignments across all your slates for a configurable number of days ahead (7, 14, 30, or 60).
- **Swap Assignments** — A section to swap rotation entries between two family members within the same slate.
- **Share Slates** — A form to share your slates with another family by entering their Family ID.

## How to Use

### Viewing Your Schedule

1. The schedule displays one block per slate, showing which family member is assigned on each date.
2. Use the day-range buttons (7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 60 days) to adjust how far ahead you look.
3. Current-day assignments are highlighted with a distinct border.

### Swapping Assignments

If you want to swap two family members' positions in a rotation:

1. In the **Swap Assignments** section, select:
   - Your Family ID (auto-filled).
   - The slate whose assignments you want to swap.
   - Two rotation entries (person + date combinations) to exchange.
2. Click **Swap Rotations**. Both members will switch positions for those dates.

### Sharing Slates with Another Family

To share your chore templates:

1. Click **Share Slate(s)** in the Share Slates section.
2. Enter the other family's Family ID.
3. Check the boxes next to the slates you want to share.
4. Click **Share**. This creates a swap meet entry that the other family can accept.

### Accepting Shared Slates

Shared slates from other families appear in your pending requests list. Review and accept them to add those slates to your own collection.
