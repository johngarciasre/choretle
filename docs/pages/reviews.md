# Reviews

The Reviews page manages jobs that require verification before they can be marked as fully complete. When a task owner submits a job for review, it appears here for approval or rejection.

## What You See

- **Filter Buttons** — Toggle between Pending, Approved, and Rejected reviews.
- **Review Cards** — Each card shows:
  - The job name.
  - Who submitted it (reviewer name).
  - Submission date.
  - Any notes attached to the review.
  - A status badge (pending, approved, rejected).

## How to Use

### Reviewing a Job

1. Find the pending review in the list.
2. Click **Review** to open the review form.
3. Add any notes or feedback about the job completion.
4. Choose one of three actions:
   - **Approve & Complete** — Mark the job as fully done and award points.
   - **Mark for Rework** — Send the job back to the Doing stage.
   - **Cancel** — Close without making changes.

### Filtering Reviews

- Use the filter buttons at the top to see only Pending, Approved, or Rejected reviews. This helps you focus on what needs attention.
