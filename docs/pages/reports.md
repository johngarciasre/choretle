# Reports

The Reports page provides a breakdown of your family's chore activity — who did what, how many points were earned, and progress over time.

## What You See

- **Tab Navigation** — Switch between Daily, Done, Task, and Member views.
- **Summary Cards** — Points Earned, Jobs Completed, and In Progress counts.
- **Completed Jobs List** — A list of recently finished jobs with their point values.
- **In Progress Jobs List** — Jobs that are currently being worked on.
- **Wallboard Toggle** — A button to show a leaderboard of all family members ranked by total points.

## How to Use

### Daily View

Shows chores completed today, along with any still-in-progress jobs. Useful for checking what happened in the last 24 hours.

### Done View

Displays all recently completed jobs regardless of when they were finished. Browse through your family's completion history.

### Task View

Groups completed jobs by task name so you can see which tasks are being done most frequently across your family.

### Member View

Shows a per-member breakdown — who completed the most chores and earned the most points recently.

### Wallboard

Click **Wallboard** to see a leaderboard of all family members sorted by total points. This is a fun way to celebrate top performers!
