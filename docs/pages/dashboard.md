# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your entry point after signing in. It provides quick access to the three main sections of the app.

## What You See

- A welcome message with your name.
- Three cards:
  - **Rotations** — View and manage chore rotation schedules.
  - **Tasks** — Manage assigned chores and tasks.
  - **Your Profile** — View your stats, streaks, and achievements.

## How to Use

1. From the Dashboard, click any card to navigate to that section.
2. Alternatively, use the top navigation bar for direct access to Tasks, Jobs, Slates, Rotations, Reports, or Family.
