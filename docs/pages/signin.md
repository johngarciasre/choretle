# Sign In / Sign Up

The authentication page at `/auth/signin` handles both signing in and creating a new account.

## How to Use

### Signing In

1. Enter your email address and password.
2. Click **Sign In**.
3. You will be redirected to the Dashboard on success.

### Creating an Account

1. Click the **Sign up** link at the bottom of the sign-in form to switch to signup mode.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** — Your display name (visible to other family members).
   - **Email** — Your email address.
   - **Password** — Choose a password.
3. Click **Sign Up**.
4. You will be redirected to the Dashboard on success.

### Remember My Email

- Check the "Remember my email" box to have your email pre-filled on future visits. This information is stored locally in your browser.

### Switching Between Sign In and Sign Up

- Toggle between modes at any time using the link at the bottom of the form ("Don't have an account? Sign up" or "Already have an account? Sign in").
